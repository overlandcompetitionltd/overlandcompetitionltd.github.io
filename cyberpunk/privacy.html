Information Collection and Use

When you download and use the Application, information is collected. This may include:

  • The Internet Protocol address assigned to your device (for example, its IP address)

Precise information about your mobile device’s location is not gathered by the Application.

  • Third-party services: anonymized location information may periodically be sent by the Service Provider to external services that assist with improvements to the Application.

The information you provide may be used by the Service Provider to contact you occasionally about important information, required notices and marketing promotions.

To improve your experience with the Application, the Service Provider may require you to supply certain personally identifiable information. Any such information will be retained and used as explained in this policy.
